# Synthetic example: meeting-to-action

Our cross-functional team holds many meetings, but decisions are slow. Ownership becomes unclear after handoffs, and important follow-ups are often lost. We want to explore whether AI could help without treating tentative discussion as a confirmed commitment.

Constraints:

- The team uses mixed Thai and English notes.
- A department lead remains accountable for final decisions.
- The first test must not write to production systems.
- Participants need a non-AI way to correct or challenge the record.
