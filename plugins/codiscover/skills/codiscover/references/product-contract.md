# CoDiscover Product Contract v0.1

## Core promise

Help any value-creating work system move from a real challenge to a responsible, prioritized, and testable Human–AI use case.

## Unit of analysis

Analyze the value-creating work system rather than department or headcount. Supported contexts include:

- a solo human coordinating AI tools or agents;
- a work cell or small team;
- a function or unit;
- an organization;
- a networked organization or ecosystem.

Universal access is achieved through adaptive paths, not one identical workflow for everyone.

## Value compass

Profile value across four independent dimensions:

1. Productivity
2. Impact
3. Inclusion
4. Innovation

Do not create a default single score. State who benefits, who carries burden or risk, and what must not become worse.

## Product modes

- Quick Discover: rapid framing, up to three candidates, trade-off snapshot, risk, and next step.
- Guided Co-Discovery: systemic mapping and transparent prioritization.
- Deep Design: conditional path from one selected use case to a bounded experiment.
- Reflect & Validate: callable checkpoint across all states.

## Five discovery lenses

Use as adaptive diagnostic lenses, not a mandatory questionnaire:

1. Entry signal
2. Work-system scope
3. Value compass
4. Human–Agent relationship
5. Horizon and evidence

## Five decision gates

1. Problem–value fit
2. Intervention choice
3. Human–AI operating model
4. Minimum testable use case
5. Go / Revise / Stop

## Locked boundaries

- Advisory only; final decisions remain human-owned.
- Production execution is outside the prototype.
- Prefer the simplest sufficient intervention, including a non-AI option.
- Record provenance as user-confirmed, AI-inferred, or unknown.
- Important decisions require an accountable human owner, review rights, and operational controls.
- Carbon emissions must not be inferred from token counts without measured or provider-verified data.
- Every framework must change a product behavior, schema field, output, or test; otherwise keep it backstage or remove it.

## Core destination

The journey ends at a decision-ready experiment, not an idea list and not a full production rollout.
