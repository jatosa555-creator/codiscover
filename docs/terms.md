# CoDiscover Terms of Use

Last updated: 18 July 2026

CoDiscover is an early decision-support prototype for discovering, comparing, and sharpening Human-AI work use cases. It does not make final organizational decisions, provide professional legal, medical, financial, or compliance advice, or authorize autonomous production action.

Users are responsible for verifying facts, protecting data, confirming rights and consent, naming an accountable human owner, and deciding whether any output should be used. Keep a practical non-AI fallback and stop a test when a critical safety, rights, accountability, or feasibility gate fails.
