# Demo Video Script — Under 3 Minutes

## Goal

Show a working CoDiscover plugin, explain how Codex and GPT-5.6 were used, and leave judges with one memorable idea: **AI use-case discovery should end in a responsible experiment, not an idea list.**

## 0:00–0:20 — Hook

**Visual:** A crowded list of generic AI ideas changes into one clear experiment card.

**Narration:**

> Most AI use-case tools generate more ideas. CoDiscover helps you make a better decision. It turns a real work challenge into a prioritized Human–AI opportunity and the smallest responsible test.

## 0:20–0:45 — Problem and difference

**Visual:** Four-value compass and the path Challenge → Use Cases → MTUC.

**Narration:**

> Organizations often start with a tool, not the work. They optimize productivity but miss impact, inclusion, innovation, ownership, and evidence. CoDiscover considers all four forms of value and keeps an accountable human in the loop.

## 0:45–1:05 — Installation and invocation

**Visual:** Repository, installation commands, then a new Codex task.

**Narration:**

> CoDiscover is a working plugin built for ChatGPT and Codex. After installation, I can invoke the skill directly with a real challenge.

**Prompt on screen:**

```text
Use $codiscover to help with this challenge:
Our cross-functional meetings produce unclear decisions,
lost follow-ups, and repeated coordination work.
```

## 1:05–1:50 — Working demo

**Visual:** CoDiscover returns a context snapshot, up to three use cases, four-value profiles, and a recommendation.

**Narration:**

> It separates facts, assumptions, and unknowns; finds leverage points; generates materially different options; includes a non-AI alternative; and explains its recommendation rather than hiding the trade-offs.

## 1:50–2:20 — Minimum Testable Use Case

**Visual:** Highlight owner, human role, AI role, checkpoints, evidence, and Go/Revise/Stop.

**Narration:**

> The output does not stop at an idea. It creates a Minimum Testable Use Case: who owns the decision, what AI may do, where humans must review, what evidence to collect, and when to go, revise, or stop.

## 2:20–2:40 — Technical and QA proof

**Visual:** Run the included QA command and show PASS.

**Narration:**

> Codex with GPT-5.6 helped transform the founder's product thinking into the plugin architecture, structured contract, validators, synthetic examples, and automated QA. Nine responsibility gates check that recommendations remain testable and accountable.

## 2:40–2:58 — Closing

**Visual:** CoDiscover wordmark and tagline.

**Narration:**

> CoDiscover is for anyone creating value with people and AI—from one person with an agent team to a complex organization. Discover the right work. Design the right relationship. Test what matters.

## Recording checklist

- Show the working plugin, not only slides.
- Keep the final video below 3:00.
- Include spoken audio explaining Codex and GPT-5.6 use.
- Avoid confidential examples or browser notifications.
- Upload as a public YouTube video.
- Verify the video while signed out before adding it to Devpost.
